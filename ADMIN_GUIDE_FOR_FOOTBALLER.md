# How to update the website (Azan)

1. Open  **https://YOUR-SITE/admin**  (save it as a bookmark on your phone)
2. Type the email and password you were given -> **Log in**

You will see 4 tabs:

## 📷 Photos
Choose one or many photos -> choose where they appear (Match / Team / Moments...) -> optional caption -> **Upload**.
Wait until the bar finishes.

## 🎬 Videos
Write a title -> date -> type (Goal / Assist...) -> **choose a video file** (up to 100 MB)
or paste a YouTube link -> **Add video**.

## ⚽ Matches
After every match: date, who you played, our goals, their goals, your goals, your assists, clean sheet -> **Add match**.
Matches, goals, assists and clean sheets on the website go up automatically.

## 🏆 Trophies
Choose Trophy / Award / Best Player -> tournament name, result, year -> **Add**.
The trophy counter goes up automatically.

Made a mistake? Press the red ✕ next to it to delete it, then add it again.
Press **Log out** when you are done.
